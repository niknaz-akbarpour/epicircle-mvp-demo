# Data and Safety Notice

This public repository is intentionally limited to a simulation-only EpiCircle demo.

## Included

- Fictional names and sample values
- Presentation-only signal percentages
- A clearly labelled sample Toronto location
- Front-end interaction logic for the five-step walkthrough

## Not included

- Patient records or protected health information
- Real seizure recordings, EEG, wearable, video, audio, GPS, or medication data
- Caregiver phone numbers or messaging credentials
- API keys, passwords, access tokens, or private infrastructure
- A trained or clinically validated seizure-detection model

## Responsible use

Do not use this demonstration to make medical decisions, change medication, replace a clinician-approved seizure action plan, or delay local emergency services. Any future research involving people or identifiable health data would require appropriate consent, governance, security, and ethics review.
